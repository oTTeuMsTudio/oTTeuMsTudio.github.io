August 2026

Why Unreal Engine, and why beginner

I spent years on 3D modeling and a stretch in other engines and tools. Unreal Engine is where I want to learn game development properly: editor, Blueprints, then C++. Calling myself a beginner is the point. The 3D artist site is retired. This studio site is the new start.

August 2026

Graybox before beauty

First maps are cubes in a pine clearing. Scale, door height, and a path the player can read. Pretty materials come after the space works. That is a 3D artist habit I am trying to keep — and a game-dev habit I am trying to learn.

August 2026

One light, then the forest

Lighting studies first: a lantern, fog, wet bark. If a scene feels like a place with a single source, I trust it more than a dozen fill lights. Next: hook that mood into a walkable blockout inside Unreal.

August 2026

From Blender files to an Unreal folder

I already know how to make game-ready meshes. The new skill is import settings, collision, Nanite choices, and materials that survive engine lighting. That is the homework for the next weeks.
