Learning in public

Journal

Notes from the Unreal Engine beginner path. Short, honest, replaceable as I ship real work.

2 September 2026

Starting a top-down strategy / shooter

The first game page is now the Games page. New recording: Unreal Engine 5.8, Blueprints, Top Down template. Click-to-move on a graybox, then the Enhanced Input actions that make the click an order. That is the start of a top-down strategy/shooter — not a trailer, an explanation of how the next game begins.

31 August 2026

First Game in Unreal

A new playable sits on the Games page. Third-person mannequin, a painted start line, colored platforms, and a timer HUD. Recorded in the Unreal Editor viewport. Packaged Android and Windows builds are on that page too. FHOU — coins and game-over from 25 August — stays underneath as the loop that started it.

26 August 2026

oTTeCAD on the site

The Rust polygonal editor now runs in the browser on a Rust page. I am writing my own 3D modeler in system-level code — mesh, GPU, tools. Same tools as the desktop build: object and edit mode, extrude, inset, gizmos, OBJ in and out. WebGPU does the viewport. It is not Blender — it is the workbench I wrote, parked next to the games.

25 August 2026

FHOU — first playable

I have a loop I can run. Third-person mannequin on a lime graybox, coins that increment a HUD, a game-over widget. Working title FHOU. Two recordings — editor and standalone preview — are still on the Games page. Pretty can wait. Play could not.

August 2026

Why Unreal Engine, and why beginner

I spent years on 3D modeling and a stretch in other engines and tools. Unreal Engine is where I want to learn game development properly: editor, Blueprints, then C++. Calling myself a beginner is the point. The 3D artist site is retired. This studio site is the new start.

August 2026

Graybox before beauty

First maps are cubes in a pine clearing. Scale, door height, and a path the player can read. Pretty materials come after the space works. That is a 3D artist habit I am trying to keep — and a game-dev habit I am trying to learn.

August 2026

One light, then the forest

Lighting studies first: a lantern, fog, wet bark. If a scene feels like a place with a single source, I trust it more than a dozen fill lights. Next: hook that mood into a walkable blockout inside Unreal.

August 2026

From Blender files to an Unreal folder

I already know how to make game-ready meshes. The new skill is import settings, collision, Nanite choices, and materials that survive engine lighting. That is the homework for the next weeks.
